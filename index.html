Open method

How this test works

01

Make a prediction

Choose an ordered sequence of three distinct numbers from 1 to 6. The codebook maps each number to a colour.

02

Lock before the draw

Your guess is fixed before the app uses the browser's cryptographic random-number generator to choose three distinct numbers uniformly at random.

03

Compare honestly

An exact ordered match has chance probability 1/120 per round. Partial matches are shown for interest, but exact matches are the primary outcome.

What this can and cannot tell us

This is an exploratory, self-run demonstration, not proof of ESP or a clinical or peer-reviewed study. Chance predicts an average of one exact hit per 120 rounds; short sessions naturally fluctuate. The displayed probability is the chance, under the stated random model, of getting at least this many exact hits, assuming the total number of rounds was fixed in advance. It is not the probability that ESP is real. Repeatedly checking results or choosing when to stop makes it unsuitable as confirmatory evidence.

The source is open for inspection, and the target is drawn only after each submitted guess. This browser-only version is not tamper-proof: a participant can modify local code or data. Treat exported results as self-reported. A confirmatory study would need a preregistered protocol, independent oversight, and server-side or otherwise audited randomization.
